Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

MoveNow Logistics respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our application, website, and transportation, delivery, and logistics services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect:

  • Contact information: Name, phone number, email address, and other details you provide.
  • Account information: Registration details and account preferences.
  • Booking information: Pickup and destination details, delivery addresses, reservations, and service history.
  • Location information: Location details needed for pickup, delivery, navigation, or tracking, where the relevant feature is enabled and permission is granted.
  • Payment information: Transaction references, payment status, and related records. Payment providers may process payment details under their own privacy policies.
  • Technical information: Device, application, diagnostic, and usage information where collected by the app or its service providers.
  • Customer support information: Messages and information you send when requesting assistance.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process transportation bookings and delivery requests.
  • Coordinate drivers, transport operators, and delivery partners.
  • Provide booking updates and customer support.
  • Process payments and maintain transaction records.
  • Help protect users, detect fraud, and improve security.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve our services.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Location Information

If you grant location permission, the app may use location information for relevant functions such as identifying pickup points, coordinating transport, or tracking deliveries. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work properly if location access is disabled.

5. How We Share Information

We may share relevant information with service providers and other parties when necessary to operate our services, including:

  • Drivers, transport operators, and delivery partners involved in a booking.
  • Payment processors and financial service providers.
  • Technology, hosting, security, and customer support providers.
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and safety.

We do not state that information is shared with every category above in every case. Sharing depends on the services and features you use.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes, protect security, and meet applicable legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the circumstances.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on the processing of your personal information. You may also manage certain device permissions through your device settings.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the official contact details provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where required, a parent or guardian should contact us regarding a child's personal information.

11. International Data Transfers

If information is processed or stored in another country, we will take steps required by applicable data protection laws for any relevant transfer.
